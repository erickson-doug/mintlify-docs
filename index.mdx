<Info>
  **Neuron 2.30.0 is now available.** Released May 21, 2026. See the [release notes](/release-notes/2.30.0) for highlights, supported versions, and upgrade guidance.

Build and run deep learning and generative AI workloads on [AWS Inferentia](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/machine-learning/inferentia/) and [AWS Trainium](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/machine-learning/trainium/) with the [AWS Neuron SDK](/about-neuron/what-is-neuron). Serve LLMs with vLLM, train and fine-tune with PyTorch or JAX, author custom kernels in NKI, or go straight to the Neuron Graph Compiler and Runtime.
